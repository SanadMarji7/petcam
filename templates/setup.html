iPad Checklist (Keeps Camera Running Unattended):

1
Wall Power & Stand: Plug the iPad in on a stand aimed at your dog's spot.
2
Auto-Lock to Never: iOS Settings → Display → Auto-Lock → Never. Turn brightness down.
3
Guided Access: iOS Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access → On. Tap Start Camera, then triple-click side button to lock Safari in place.
