Before scanning

  1. Make sure the device is online (Wi-Fi or mobile data). Nothing needs to be installed: the pages run in the browser.
  2. On an iPhone or iPad, add the page to the Home Screen afterwards (Share → Add to Home Screen). Safari forgets the stored key of an ordinary tab after a week without use; a Home Screen app keeps it.
  3. On the iPad: Settings → Display & Brightness → Auto-Lock → Never.
  4. On the iPad: Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access → On. After the camera starts, triple-click the top button to lock Safari in place.
